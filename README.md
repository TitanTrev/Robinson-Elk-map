# Robinson Elk Map

Interactive map of Oregon elk hunting units for the Robinson hunting party.

**Open it:** https://titantrev.github.io/robinson-elk-map/

Tap any unit to see:
- ODFW elk population estimate, management objective, and bull/calf ratios (2022–2026)
- Elk harvest by hunt type for 2021–2025, plus every individual hunt
- Wolf packs ODFW labels in or near the unit, with year-end 2025 counts

Base maps: USGS Topo, satellite, USGS imagery + topo, Esri Topo, OpenTopoMap, shaded relief.
Overlays: Forest Service roads, MVUM roads and trails, Forest Service trails, land ownership, wolf use areas.

The map needs a data connection to load map tiles. Download offline maps in onX, Gaia, or CalTopo before heading out.

## Data sources
- ODFW Big Game Population Survey Data (Rocky Mountain and Roosevelt elk, 2022–2026)
- ODFW Big Game Harvest Statistics (Any Legal Weapon, Archery, Muzzleloader elk; 2021–2025). ODFW still marks the 2024 reports as drafts.
- ODFW Areas of Known Wolf Activity statewide map (Dec 31, 2025), 2025 Annual Wolf Report, and county wolf pages. Wolf areas were traced from ODFW's PDF map and are approximate.
- ODFW Wildlife Management Units (Oregon GeoHub)

Data compiled October 24, 2026.
